### 0734 — Cross-platform naming policy landed (TASK-260715-1ffbkg)
- MILESTONE: `gramdrive_model::naming` — `sanitize` (total: no input has a failure mode) + `resolve_siblings` (collision-free sibling set). `crates/gramdrive-model/src/naming.rs`. SYNC-012, SYNC-013, PLAT-021, POL-1. `make check` 8/8; 83/83 tests in gramdrive-model, 37 new: 7 unit, 6 fixture (over a 53-case corpus table), 15 collision, 9 property.
- DECISION: **one name for the strictest target, not one per platform.** A per-platform name would make one chat a different path per device and break `chat.json` links (SYNC-032). `Platform::check` models each of the four faithfully so the corpus asserts a single output satisfies all; the policy is their union plus GramDrive-only rules (NFC, no invisibles) that no filesystem enforces.
- DECISION: pipeline order is load-bearing — strip invisibles **before** NFC. Deleting a control from between a base char and its combining mark leaves a sequence that composes; normalizing first emits a non-NFC name. Pinned by corpus case "control between base and combining mark" (`Cafe\u{7}\u{301}` → `Café`).
- DECISION: ZWJ (U+200D) and ZWNJ (U+200C) are **kept** though they sit inside the stripped zero-width range. Stripping ZWJ tears 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 into separate people; stripping ZWNJ corrupts Persian/Indic. Bidi overrides/isolates/LRM/RLM are stripped — spoofing vector (`photo\u{202e}gnp.exe` renders as `photoexe.png`), not a display preference.
- DECISION: truncation cuts at **grapheme-cluster** boundaries, not codepoints — a codepoint cut halves a flag into a stray regional indicator and leaves dangling ZWJs. Codepoint fallback only for a single cluster larger than the whole budget (zalgo); `compose` re-normalizes after that path.
- DECISION: collision suffix = base32 of FNV-1a + splitmix64 finalizer over `ItemId` bytes — **not** an id prefix. Sibling ids share long prefixes (version, kind tag, account scope) and differ deep in the payload, so a prefix would be identical across the very items being distinguished. Non-cryptographic on purpose: a forced digest collision is just another collision, absorbed deterministically by escalation (7 chars → 13 → full id text, which cannot collide).
